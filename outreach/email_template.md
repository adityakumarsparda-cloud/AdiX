## Rules before you hit send

- Replace every bracket. An unedited `[Company]` reads worse than no email.
- The company-specific line is what makes this not a mass mail. If you cannot
  write one that is true, send it without the line rather than faking it.
- One person per email. Never BCC a list.
- Max 20 new outreach emails a day.
- Never email the same person twice for the same role without a reply.
- Check `Do Not Contact` in the tracker first.
